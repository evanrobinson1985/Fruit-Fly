    Credits & citations

    This simulation is assembled from other people's research. Please cite their work.

    Connectome: MaleCNS v1.0

    The neurons, connections, neurotransmitter predictions, cell types and positions are from the complete male Drosophila CNS connectome, produced by the HHMI Janelia FlyEM project team with Google Research, the Cambridge Connectomics Group and MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology (Jefferis group), and an international proofreading consortium. Berg, S. et al. Sexual dimorphism in the complete Drosophila male central nervous system connectome. Cell (2026). Released under CC-BY 4.0. Full author list and citation: male-cns.janelia.org.

    Neuron model

    Shiu, P. K., Sterne, G. R., Spiller, N., Franconville, R., Sandoval, A., Zhou, J., Simha, N., Kang, C. H., Yu, S., Kim, J. S., Dorkenwald, S., Matsliah, A., Schlegel, P., Yu, S.-c., McKellar, C. E., Sterling, A., Costa, M., Eichler, K., Bates, A. S., Eckstein, N., Funke, J., Jefferis, G. S. X. E., Murthy, M., Bidaye, S. S., Hampel, S., Seeds, A. M. & Scott, K. A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing. Nature 634, 210–219 (2024). doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9. Short-term depression, the connectome edits and the browser engine were added by this project.

    3D fly: FlyBody

    Vaxenburg, R., Siwanowicz, I., Merel, J., Robie, A. A., Morrow, C., Novati, G., Stefanidi, Z., Both, G.-J., Card, G. M., Reiser, M. B., Botvinick, M. M., Branson, K. M., Tassa, Y. & Turaga, S. C. Meshes and skeleton from TuragaLab/flybody, Apache License 2.0; re-encoded for the web, geometry unchanged.

    Rigging and recorded leg movements: NeuroMechFly / flygym

    Wang-Chen, S., Stimpfling, V. A., Lam, T. K. C., Özdil, P. G., Genoud, L., Hurtak, F. & Ramdya, P. NeuroMechFly v2: simulating embodied sensorimotor control in adult Drosophila. Nature Methods 21, 2353–2362 (2024). doi:10.1038/s41592-024-02497-y.

    Lobato-Rios, V., Ramalingasetty, S. T., Özdil, P. G., Arreguit, J., Ijspeert, A. J. & Ramdya, P. NeuroMechFly, a neuromechanical model of adult Drosophila melanogaster. Nature Methods 19, 620–627 (2022). doi:10.1038/s41592-022-01466-7. Joint rigging and the single-step leg trajectories (recorded from a real fly walking on a ball) come from NeLy-EPFL/flygym, Apache License 2.0.

    Which neurons sense and act

    Vinegar glomeruli: Semmelhack & Wang, Nature 2009. CO₂: Suh et al., Nature 2004. Geosmin: Stensmyr et al., Cell 2012. LC4 and LPLC2 looming detectors: von Reyn et al., Nat. Neurosci. 2017; Ache et al., Curr. Biol. 2019. Giant Fiber escape: von Reyn et al., Nat. Neurosci. 2014; Card & Dickinson, Curr. Biol. 2008. P9 forward walking: Bidaye et al., Neuron 2020. Moonwalker backward walking: Bidaye et al., Science 2014. DNa01/DNa02 steering: Rayshubskiy et al., bioRxiv 2020; Yang et al., Cell 2024. MN9 proboscis extension: Gordon & Scott, Neuron 2009. Synaptic depression: Kazama & Wilson, Neuron 2008. Spontaneous smell-receptor firing: de Bruyne, Foster & Carlson, Neuron 2001. Long-mode and directional takeoffs (DNp02, DNp04, DNp11): von Reyn et al., Nat. Neurosci. 2014; Peek, PhD thesis, U. Chicago 2018. Landing (DNp07, DNp10): Ache et al., Nat. Neurosci. 2019. Wingbeat amplitude (DNg02): Namiki et al., Curr. Biol. 2022. Flight saccades (DNb01): Schretter et al., bioRxiv 2023. Courtship song (pIP10, pMP2): von Philipsborn et al., Neuron 2011. Head grooming (DNg07, DNg08): Cande et al., eLife 2018. Grooming: DNg12 drives head sweeps alternating with front-leg rubbing, DNg11 leg rubbing (Guo, Zhang & Simpson, Curr. Biol. 2022); antennal grooming circuit: Hampel et al., eLife 2015. Motor-neuron pools, touch and wind neurons are taken from the MaleCNS annotations.
